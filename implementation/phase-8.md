#### Full Input Arbitration & IrrlichtUIBackend

- [ ] **Full input arbitration** (6-priority chain) implemented in `UIManager::onEvent()`:
  - **Pre-Priority-1 (unconditional)** — `WindowFocusGained` and `WindowFocusLost` events MUST be detected at the top of `UIManager::onEvent()` and returned `false` (pass-through to `CameraController`) immediately, before any priority-level evaluation. These events are NEVER consumed by any priority level — even during blocking modal active. Failure to forward them prevents `CameraController::m_appHasFocus` from updating, breaking edge-scroll and input recovery after Alt+Tab. (ref: `architecture/ui-ux/input-arbitration.md`)
  - **Priority 1** — Blocking modal active: scrim consumes left-click + right-click; camera pass-through for scroll/MMB/RMB drag; Escape consumed by modal (does not open pause menu)
  - **Priority 2** — CRITICAL toast dismiss (`hasCriticalToastVisible()` + no blocking modal): click/Enter/Delete routed to `NotificationManager::dismissCriticalToast()`; dual guard (both conditions evaluated independently per `architecture/ui-ux/input-arbitration.md`): `(hasCriticalToastVisible() && !isModalActive())`; checking only `!criticalVisible` is insufficient — the `!isModalActive()` guard is equally mandatory and must not be omitted or short-circuited
  - **Priority 3** — QueryPanel / Inspector panel: outside-click closes panel and is consumed (preventing accidental zone placement); toolbar and minimap carve-out clicks are NOT consumed (pass-through to toolbar/minimap handler); Escape consumed by QueryPanel when open; Ctrl+Z passes through to Priority 5; mutual exclusion with Tax Rate Panel enforced here (opening QueryPanel closes Tax Rate Panel)
  - **Priority 4** — Tax Rate Panel (when open): consumes all clicks within its bounds and consumes Escape to close itself; clicks outside the panel bounds are NOT consumed (pass-through, allowing camera pan while the panel is open); Ctrl+Z passes through to Priority 5; mutual exclusion: opening Tax Rate Panel closes QueryPanel
  - **Priority 5** — UIManager HUD controls: toolbar click, speed selector, undo button, minimap click, bell icon; Ctrl+Z undo processed at this priority level
  - **Priority 6** — `CameraController::onInputEvent()`: pan/zoom/rotate/edge-scroll (lowest priority — only receives unconsumed events); simulation tool actions (zone/road/demolish placement) fall through after Priority 6 to the game world
  (ref: `architecture/ui-ux/input-arbitration.md`, `architecture/ui-ux/ui-manager.md`)
